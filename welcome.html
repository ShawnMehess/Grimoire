Character Vault

A private place to build and run your D&D 5e characters.

Open the Character Vault

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your Google account, using the sign-in button in the top corner of the vault page.
  2. Click + New Character at the top of your list of characters.
  3. Work through the creation wizard, then finish setup.

Before you start: ask your DM

Ask your DM what rules they want before you pick, because everything after that depends on it.

The first wizard step is where you choose which books your character is built from, and that limits what the later steps can offer you. You can change the books later, but anything you picked that only existed in a removed book gets cleared, so it asks you first.

What the wizard asks you

  1. Rules & Sources. Pick your books, and how hit points get worked out.
  2. Identity. Name, starting level, and species, plus whatever that species grants.
  3. Class. What your character does best. Sets hit points, attacks and features.
  4. Background. Where they come from. Grants proficiencies and starting gear.
  5. Story. Two free-text boxes for appearance and backstory. Both optional, editable later.
  6. Starting Conditions. Your six ability scores, and your starting equipment.
  7. Review. Check what you picked, then finish setup. Nothing is locked in; every choice stays editable.

After you finish setup

You get a character sheet you can rearrange as you like, split across tabs: Main, Character, and Leveling. Come back to the Leveling tab when your character levels up.

There are display options too: Simple View stacks every block full width in reading order, and there are theme choices if you want a different look.

Using it on different devices

It works on a laptop, a tablet and a phone, and the page adapts to whichever screen you are on. There is a print stylesheet too, so a printed sheet comes out black on white rather than as a dark screen.
